Logo application.

One geometry, recoloured through CSS tokens, and one rule for both variants: every strand comes from the product palette. Light surfaces pair Midnight Navy with Waqfa deep green; dark surfaces pair Soft Gray with Sage Teal; the identity gold centre never changes.

On light surfaces

Midnight Navy (16.17:1 here) and brand deep green (10.77:1) form the strands, with clearer ring separation than the previous charcoal. WCAG exempts logotypes from contrast minimums; both strands clear AAA regardless.

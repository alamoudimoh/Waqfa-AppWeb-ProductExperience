01

Brand Philosophy & Pillars

🕌

Waqf Dedication · وقف

Waqfa exists as an endowment — هذا التطبيق وقفٌ لله تعالى. No monetisation pressure shapes the interface: no ads, no dark patterns, no engagement traps. The design serves the worshipper, nothing else.

🕊

Sakina · سكينة

Clarity before action. Deep emerald and warm ivory carry calm; gold marks only what matters — the logo centre, the primary action, the moment of focus. Low visual noise is a spiritual requirement, not a style choice.

⚖

Amanah · أمانة

Trusteeship in both directions: every religious text passes the review committee (اللجنة) before entering the app, and every colour pair is contrast-measured at source — the palette documents its own WCAG ratios and never claims more than it delivers.
